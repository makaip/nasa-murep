## 0. Data and common setup

- Data: `E:\satdata\Custom\yucatan_sst_chlor_cdomslope_ssl_bathy_2010-2019_v4.pkl` (v4). Variables: sst, ssl, chlorophyll, cdom_412, cdom_slope_275_295, cdom_slope_300_600 (+ `*_n_points` scalars); metadata has lat/lon centres+edges, bbox, bathymetry (GEBCO). DataFrame 3653 days x 12 columns, 2010-01-01 .. 2020-01-01; trimmed to 2010-2019 -> 3652 days (2010-01-01 .. 2019-12-31), grid 250x250.
- Daily valid-pixel fraction (whole grid): sst 72.5%, ssl 100.0%, chlorophyll 26.5%, cdom_412 26.4%, cdom_slope_275_295 26.5%, cdom_slope_300_600 26.5%.
- Depth mask: GEBCO bathymetry <= -10 m -> 41,144 of 62,500 pixels. The Yucatan bbox used by the existing figure scripts contains all of these, so the stats domain is simply all deep pixels.
- One zone map per month-year (from monthly-mean fields) + 12 climatology maps. Daily pixels are labelled by their own month-year map; shallow / unclassified pixels are excluded from both inside and outside.
- IF model: `iforest_pipeline5.joblib` (n_estimators=200, contamination=auto, random_state=42, Yeo-Johnson on [sst_anom, ssl_anom]) - matches train.py, so it was reused (no retrain). Run in the repo `.venv` (scikit-learn 1.7.2, the version the model was saved with).
- a_CDOM(412): v4 contains **no negative values** (min 7e-6 m^-1). The MLR output is exp(ln a) and preprocessing keeps only 0 <= a <= 1 (`pickler_config.MIN_CDOM/MAX_CDOM`), so 'keep negatives' is a no-op here.

## 5. Interpretation notes and issues found

1. **The a_CDOM-Chl correlation is mostly built into the algorithms.** `pickler_utils.calculate_cdom` / `calculate_cdom_slopes`
   compute ln a412 = -3.070 - 1.285 ln Rrs443 + 1.107 ln Rrs547, and ln S275 and ln S300 use the same two bands with
   opposite-sign coefficients (B1 ~ -B2). All three CDOM products are therefore near-monotonic functions of the
   Rrs443/Rrs547 ratio. MODIS Chl-a (OCI/OC3M) is also a blue/green band-ratio algorithm. That explains why:
   (a) a412, S275, S300 and log Chl give the same |Cliff's delta| in every month and method (section 3);
   (b) rho(a412, Chl) is about 0.97 everywhere, inside and outside;
   (c) the log-log scatter has a sharp kink near Chl ~ 0.3 mg/m3 and a412 ~ 0.04-0.05 1/m, where the two
   algorithms' functional forms differ (OC3M's max-band switch, the CI/OCx blend at low Chl).
   The pattern the supervisor saw (strong at low CDOM, tapering higher up) is real in the data (quartile rho 0.67,
   0.78, 0.96, 0.87; upper-half rho inside the zone 0.73-0.84 vs lower half 0.96-0.98). It is best described as a
   property of the retrieval algorithms, not independent biogeochemical evidence. Recommend saying this explicitly

2. **Inside vs outside differences are large and consistent where a zone exists.** For May-Sep, IF zone pixels have

3. **Does correlation differ inside vs outside?** -1 sigma: no meaningful difference (paired month-year Wilcoxon

   meaningless at n ~ 10^6-10^7 autocorrelated pixel-days.

**-1 sigma rule actually implemented (redo/sigma.py).** For each month-year (y, m), let T_ym(p) be the mean of the daily MUR L4 SST
(`analysed_sst`, v4 pickle) at pixel p over all days of that month. Let D = {p : GEBCO depth >= 10 m and T_ym(p) finite}. Then
mu_ym = mean over D of T_ym(p), sd_ym = population sd (ddof 0) over D of T_ym(p), and p is in the zone if p in D and T_ym(p) < mu_ym - sd_ym.
It is a spatial threshold within each monthly-mean map, applied per month-year. Flagged: **12.87%** of deep pixel-months.

Paper wording vs rules (numbers on v4 from `redo/sigma_variants.py`):

**a_CDOM(412) and slope retrieval / clipping** (`data/preprocessing/pickler_utils.py`, coefficients `pickler_config.py`):

The Bretherton n_eff is extremely conservative here (spatial lag-1 rank autocorrelation ~0.99 gives n_eff of tens out of 10^5-10^7) and is only usable for the pooled scopes.

**Recommendation for the paper:** report c1 (block bootstrap over whole month-years, 95% CI of rho_in - rho_out), supported by d (Wilcoxon over month-years). Both use the month-year, the unit at which zones are defined, as the independent sample, and neither needs a parametric autocorrelation model. Do not report naive p-values. Result: IF, rho is lower inside than outside, all months -0.035 [95% CI -0.049, -0.023], May-Sep -0.056 [-0.073, -0.040], p < 0.001 (Wilcoxon p = 2e-14, 3e-12; still p = 0.014 / 0.037 under the very conservative n_eff). -1 sigma, no difference pooled (-0.002 [-0.006, +0.001]); May-Sep +0.009 [+0.004, +0.014], negligible. The IF difference is statistically robust but small (rho 0.92 vs 0.97). It is consistent with the IF zone sitting in the high-a_CDOM range, where the algorithmic a_CDOM-Chl relationship bends (section 5), so it should not be read as a distinct biogeochemical regime.
